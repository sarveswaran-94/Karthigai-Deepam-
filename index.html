XR Karthikai Deepam

Place small clay oil lamps around your real room in passthrough.

  • Step 1 – Kolam: point at the floor and pinch / press trigger to lay the 2 ft kolam (stick turns it). Then lamps unlock.
  • Pinch / trigger on empty space – place a lamp (up to 40)
  • Pinch / trigger and hold on a lamp – carry it, release to drop it somewhere else
  • Quick tap on a lamp – turn it 45°  ·  thumbstick left/right turns it smoothly
  • Thumbstick up/down or the small − / + panel – darker / clearer room (starts dark)
  • A / X – blow out or relight  ·  B / Y – remove  ·  hands: pinch a lamp and hold still 1.5 s to remove
  • Stick click – Touch mode: lamp goes exactly at your controller tip
